# Voco product page

The website is a Persuade surface for Mac users who want to write by speaking. Its primary action is downloading Voco. The existing app identity remains authoritative for the logo, graphite and orange palette, Manrope typography, and soft-white primary controls.

## Direction contract

THESIS. Show an idea taking shape as an original orange ASCII ribbon, then explain the shortcut and prove the product with real app captures.

OWN-WORLD. Warm white text on graphite, a single orange voice accent, generous space, thin dividers, and restrained rounded controls. Preserve the brand mark's proportions.

STORY. The visitor understands local English dictation, watches the completed-text insertion example, explores the actual app, checks privacy and requirements, then downloads an early-access build.

FIRST VIEWPORT. A large two-line headline and download action sit beside an animated folded ribbon. Mobile stacks the ribbon below the copy. It is decorative artwork, not microphone activity.

FORM. Code-led product page, selected under the user's explicit instruction to choose a reference. Maple Research's ASCII posters on Vibrant supplied the visual reference. ByDefault supplied a comparison for product clarity. No generated comp or random concept seed was used.

FINISH. Verify the built page at desktop, mobile, and narrow widths; review the actual captures; check the published download and production owner. Review and limitations are recorded in verification.md.

## Asset provenance

- The logo and app icon come from the committed Voco brand assets.
- Manrope and its license come from the app's bundled font assets.
- App screenshots come from the current built renderer via `website/scripts/capture-app.cjs`. They retain the sample-data preview notices.
- The social image is a code-authored composition generated by `website/scripts/capture-social.cjs` with those assets.
- The animated ribbon is original canvas geometry. No reference-site image or illustration is shipped.
- Text reveal and FAQ motion use the individual transitions.dev recipes. Their snippet CSS remains verbatim, with required reduced-motion guards.

## Architecture

`website/` has its own package and Vite build. Static HTML keeps the product copy, real download, and expanded FAQ available without JavaScript. A small interaction module owns navigation, demo phases, gallery selection, and release metadata. A separate canvas module owns the ribbon and its visibility lifecycle. The desktop application's runtime and package dependencies do not depend on the website.

The alternative was a React or Astro application. Static HTML and vanilla JavaScript keep this one-page product site smaller and avoid adding a framework boundary that the content does not need.
